Economy and markets.

Tariffs. Mechanism: the administration is preparing higher tariffs on sixteen economies including China and the European Union, following its Section three-oh-one probe into excess factory capacity; rates are not yet reported. Intended effect: rebuild leverage after the Supreme Court struck down much of the earlier programme. Risks: protectionists say overcapacity abroad justifies it; free-traders say it lifts import prices into an energy-driven inflation spike and invites retaliation, and the EU wants its fifteen percent deal ceiling respected. Read-through: more goods inflation complicates the Fed's December eighth and ninth meeting, props the dollar, and hits Asia's tech supply chain.
