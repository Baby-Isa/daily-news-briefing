Economy and markets.

AI servers. The price rises hit trading firms hardest, per the Financial Times. Mechanism: suppliers reprice signed orders as component costs climb. Intended effect: protect margins. Risk: one school reads it as demand outrunning supply; another as a late-cycle squeeze on customers whose returns on compute are unproven. Read-through: the cost side of the capex cycle is rising just as financing tightens, which pressures the customers of your largest holdings.

Argentina. Borrowing costs jumped as Milei's falling approval ahead of his re-election bid rattles investors: a political risk premium, priced before any policy changes.
