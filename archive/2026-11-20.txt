Economy and markets.

Brazil. Mechanism: the central bank sold one billion dollars of reserves as the dollar neared five point nine three reais. Intended effect: supply dollars and stall depreciation. Risk: relief faded by afternoon because the problem is fiscal; markets distrust Lula's adjustment, so the fiscal-dominance reading says intervention cannot fix a risk premium, only credible budgets or higher rates can. Read-through: dollar strength is squeezing emerging markets, and Mexico's peso broke nineteen per dollar, weakest in seventeen months.

Rates. IMF chief Georgieva says artificial intelligence demand will force major central banks to raise rates. The logic is a higher neutral rate from investment demand; sceptics expect productivity gains to be disinflationary later. Markets are pricing the first reading: Asian tech fell even as Anthropic closed its first day up seventeen percent at ninety-seven dollars, a one point three trillion dollar valuation, after raising eighty-one billion. Nvidia sits near one hundred fifty dollars, nearly forty percent below its October peak.
