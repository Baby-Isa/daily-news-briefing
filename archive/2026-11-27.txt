Economy and markets.

Chips. Mechanism: a new US rule requires licences for firms in four South-East Asian countries to buy advanced AI chips, and Nvidia has reportedly paused shipments. Intended effect: close the route by which restricted chips reach Chinese customers. Risks: one school says licensing is the only way to enforce controls that smuggling has hollowed out; another says it diverts rather than stops trade and pushes buyers towards Huawei, costing Nvidia either way. Read-through: Nvidia is your largest look-through exposure, and Asian data-centre builds face delay.

Fed. Markets treat a December hike as the base case; the Financial Times says the argument is a three-way tussle over inflation, AI and guidance.
