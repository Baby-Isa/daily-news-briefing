Economy and markets.

China. Exports rose thirty-six percent on the year as AI-related shipments boomed, after twenty-five in August; imports rose a third as dearer oil swelled the energy bill. The AI capex cycle is showing up as Chinese hardware exports, while the oil shock taxes importers.

Japan. Banks and insurers are returning to domestic bonds as yields surge. Mechanism: higher Japanese yields raise the hurdle for buying foreign debt. Intended: cheaper funding for Japanese lenders at home. Risk: it removes a marginal Treasury buyer just as the IMF flags the biggest top-economy deficits since Covid; economists split on whether hedging costs, not yields, drive these flows. Read-through: a firmer yen and a higher US term premium, a headwind for long-duration tech.
