Economy. Payrolls. The US added 162,000 jobs in August against a consensus of 53,000, with June and July revised up by 55,000 and unemployment steady at four point one percent. The twelve-month average was 31,000, so this is a break in trend, not noise. Four parts. Mechanism: no lever moved, but the data moved the Fed's reaction function. As flagged yesterday, Waller said he could back a hold on the fifteenth if Friday's CPI cools and would consider a hike if it does not; this pushes him toward the hike. Intent: Warsh's committee wants demand restrained enough to reach two percent while an oil and diesel shock drags headline the other way. The risk is where the schools split. On a Phillips-curve reading, a labour market this tight lets the energy shock generalise into wages, so you hike. On a supply-shock reading you are tightening into a relative-price move rates cannot fix, and you buy a recession. Private employers added only 38,000 of the 162,000, the best evidence for that second camp.
