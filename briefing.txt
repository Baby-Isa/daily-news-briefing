Politics and government.

Europe's fiscal bind. Defence spending has nearly doubled in six years, to four hundred fifty-four billion euros, and the three point five percent of output target by twenty thirty-five would rival the Marshall Plan. Meanwhile ageing pensions crowd out other spending, fuelling this weekend's French protests. Keynesians read the defence outlay as growth support; sceptics see crowding-out and higher borrowing.

Russia's budget. The finance ministry wants the central bank to report accounts regularly receiving personal transfers to the tax service, hunting undeclared income as war spending bites.

Turkey. Erdogan struggles with a scandal over investment funds allegedly used to inflate share prices.
