Warsh. His first Jackson Hole speech as chair is tomorrow. The mechanism: he has already stripped forward guidance from the statement and rate forecasts from the dot plot, so the speech is the only guidance instrument left, three weeks before a September meeting markets price at roughly one-in-three for a rise. The intent is to re-anchor expectations through credibility rather than promises. The risk splits: one school says removing guidance restores optionality and ends the habit of pre-commitment; the other says that with core inflation still elevated on energy in July, silence raises the term premium instead of lowering inflation expectations. The read-through: with the Treasury bidding for its own long end, a hawkish Warsh means a steeper curve, a firmer dollar against sterling, and a higher discount rate on exactly the capital spending Nvidia just validated.
