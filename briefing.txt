Economy. American inflation was three point four percent in July, cooling on petrol and groceries, housing still firm. The Federal Reserve is debating a rise, not a cut, and this print buys it time. Mechanism: nothing moved, and that is the point. Holding the funds rate while expected inflation falls tightens policy passively. Intent: keep expectations anchored through an energy shock without paying for it in employment. Risk: this is a supply shock, not demand. The Iran war put a dollar a gallon on petrol, and a Phillips curve reading says tightening into that destroys jobs to fix prices monetary policy cannot reach, while the credibility school says a bank that waves energy through gets it back as wages. Labour demand is already softening, employers have gone shy of hiring, and credit card balances have reached one point two six trillion. Read-through: September hike odds have fallen, the dollar is flat, and cheaper money at the margin supports AI capital expenditure, which needs a low discount rate more than it needs earnings.
