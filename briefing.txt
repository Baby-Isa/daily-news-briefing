Mechanism: September's quarter-point hike took rates to three point seven five to four percent, and minutes show most officials expect another before year-end; no pre-commitment keeps the twenty-seventh to twenty-eighth October meeting live. Intended effect: let three point seven percent inflation and oil pass-through settle without a signal moving markets. Risk: monetarists say silence invites unanchored expectations; Keynesians say hiking into an oil shock deepens the slowdown.
