Politics and government.

The Justice Department asked an appeals court to speed its suit over up to twenty thousand California mail ballots it says went to noncitizens. Economy and markets.

Tech sell-off. The Nasdaq fell two point six percent Thursday as the AI bubble debate intensified; the yen and Japanese bonds rallied on flight to safety. The digest names no single trigger. California's pension fund Calpers cut its Magnificent Seven holdings, and OpenAI trimmed its valuation target to one point two five trillion dollars from one point four. Two readings. Rates: the Fed hiked in September, and higher discount rates compress long-duration equities whatever AI does. Capex: doubts about returns on AI spending hit chip suppliers and their lenders first. Wednesday's Fed decision is the test; the UK Budget lands the same day.

Mexico: the peso is at a one-year low, down nearly ten percent since early September. The Fed hiked while Banxico held, narrowing the rate gap that pays carry traders to hold pesos.

UK: consumer confidence fell at its fastest rate since Covid. South Africa: fuel retailers warn the levy cut falls short. Asia: Singapore's core inflation eased to two percent; South Korean growth likely picked up on chip exports, a Reuters poll says.
