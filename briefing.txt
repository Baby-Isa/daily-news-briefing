Economy and markets.

Farm aid. Trump announced eight billion dollars at a Kansas rally. Mechanism: one-off cash payments offsetting diesel and fertiliser costs from the Hormuz disruption. Intended effect: rural margins and votes before the third of November. Risk: it treats the symptom; a former Agriculture Department economist calls it temporary and transparently electoral, and it adds Treasury supply beside a record interest bill. Read-through: more issuance into a market already charging a term premium, and no relief on the input prices feeding inflation.

UK. Unemployment fell to four point seven percent, a surprise, but small firms are cutting hiring, citing labour costs. Phillips-curve reading: no loosening of wage pressure, so little room for the Bank of England to ease, supportive of sterling. The Budget and the Fed decision both land next Wednesday.
