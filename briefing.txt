Economy and markets.

Russia sanctions. Trump waived the new sanctions law for sixty days by executive order; Rubio says the pause lets Washington analyse the effect on the global and American economy. Mechanism: sanctions and tariffs Congress just passed are switched off. Intended effect: more Russian oil and diesel reaching a market with Brent near one hundred ten dollars, easing inflation before the Fed meets on the twenty-seventh and twenty-eighth. Risks: Kpler says relief is unlikely to ease the tanker crisis; Zelensky calls it a reward for Putin; Republican allies call it a mistake. Read-through: oil stays the swing variable for the hike debate, so no relief yet for rate-sensitive tech.

AI capex. The FT runs two readings. One: America's ten trillion dollar AI infrastructure boom is fraying against physical economics. Two: ninety-four economists see an AI-driven surge reaching beyond the US and China. Both fit a Solow-paradox story: costs land first, productivity later.
