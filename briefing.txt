Economy and markets.

Bond fund managers are pausing ahead of Tuesday's midterms and Friday's payrolls, the Financial Times reports. Mechanism: thin positioning means data lands on an empty book and moves yields more than the news deserves. Refining. Oil majors refuse to build refineries despite war-inflated margins. The textbook says high margins attract capacity. Their answer: margins collapse at a ceasefire, and a plant is a decades-long bet under policy risk. Result: diesel stays dear, feeding the inflation central banks are fighting.
