Economy and markets.

IMF outlook. Global growth is forecast at three point one percent this year, up from three, and inflation at four point seven. AI investment offsets the war's energy shock; only thirty-two countries now generate ninety-five percent of growth. The risk: if AI profit hopes are reassessed as yields rise, global output falls one point one percent by twenty twenty-eight, America's one point eight. It also warns of the biggest deficits among top economies since Covid.

Diesel tax. Trump suspended federal diesel taxes temporarily. Mechanism: a per-gallon levy removed. Intended: cheaper farm and freight costs before the midterms. Risk: lost revenue widens a deficit already pushing yields up, and on a supply-constrained fuel the gain may accrue to producers; farmers call it marginal. Read-through: fiscal loosening against hawkish central banks keeps term premia high.
