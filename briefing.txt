Economy.

The Bank of Japan is reported to be preparing a September hike and a faster tightening path. Four parts.

Mechanism: the policy rate rises off a deeply negative real level, signalling that subsequent steps come quicker, against producer-price pressure and a yen near 160.

Intent: to break the carry trade. Traders used the July intervention itself to rebuild yen shorts, so intervention has failed and only rate differentials will close it.

Risks: monetarists say Japan is late and inflation is entrenched. The stagnationist reading says domestic demand is not the binding constraint and hiking into a tariff-hit export sector buys deflation back. The awkward part, flagged earlier this week, is that Washington quietly benefits from a cheap yen, because yen funding is one of the pipes feeding the American technology boom.

Read-through: a real tightening cycle raises the global cost of the cheapest funding currency, pressuring leveraged positions in exactly the US megacap and AI capital-expenditure names that dominate this portfolio. Watch September.

Tariffs. The White House says evasion is costing nineteen to twenty-six billion a year, accusing China of routing exports through forty-plus countries and naming nine Latin American states. A hundred percent tariff now applies to many drones and all aircraft parts; fifty percent on Canada lands this month. The Laffer problem in miniature: rates high enough that the marginal revenue comes from enforcement, not the rate.

AI capital cycle. OpenAI and Anthropic have cut prices in a real price war as Chinese models close the gap, compressing the margin assumption underneath the capex. Chinese technology valuations now trade at multiples of US peers. Goldman is marketing the Nvidia financing we flagged. Memory is the live constraint: DRAM up over fifty percent in a quarter, quadrupled since autumn.

UK growth beat expectations on heat and World Cup spending, neither durable. Colombia declared an economic emergency with ninety-six percent of its borrowing quota used, so reconstruction has no fiscal space.
