Economy and markets.

Bonds. The sell-off intensified: US ten-year yields hit a twenty-four-year high, British thirty-year gilts topped six percent for the first time since nineteen ninety-eight, French ten-year yields neared five percent, highest since two thousand two. The oddity is that Fed hike odds fell this week, so this is term premium, not the policy path. One reading: deficits repriced, fiscal dominance. The other: oil-driven inflation, which New York Fed president Williams does not see at two percent until twenty twenty-eight, with consumer confidence at a twelve-year low. Either way, long-duration tech valuations and sterling suffer.
