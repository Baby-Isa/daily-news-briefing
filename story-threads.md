# Story threads: things to watch

The short list that lets tomorrow's brief pick up a thread from an earlier
one. Read it, and the past week of briefs in `archive/`, before drafting.

Rules (changed 21 November 2026; the old file had grown to 160 KB of
appended daily notes and was being read only in part):

- REWRITE this list in full every day. Never append a dated block.
- At most twenty entries, newest first, each ONE line in exactly this form:

      - [expires YYYY-MM-DD] Topic | said Fri 20 Nov: what the brief said | watch: what would change it

- Expiry is seven days after the entry was last updated, or the day after
  a dated event it is waiting for, whichever is later. Drop anything
  resolved.
- This file holds open stories only. Web searches, unverified items,
  weather and gadget notes belong in the drafter's report, not here.
- `tools/housekeeping.py` enforces all of this before every commit: it
  deletes expired or malformed entries and trims long ones.
