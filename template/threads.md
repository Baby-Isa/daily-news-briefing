# Things to watch

A short list of open stories, so that tomorrow's briefing can pick up a
thread from an earlier one. The drafter rewrites this file in full every
day; `tools/housekeeping.py` then drops expired entries and keeps at most
twelve. One line per entry, newest first, in exactly this form:

    - [expires YYYY-MM-DD] Topic | said Thu 19 Nov: what the brief said | watch: what would change it
