## Sections

### Politics
<!-- EDIT: onboarding section 1 -->
Included: the major political headlines; the UK; the United States; Europe.
Treat process stories and cabinet noise as churn: one line or nothing.
Politics with a mechanism attached (a law passed, a budget measure, a
government falling) gets proper treatment.

### Conflict and world affairs
<!-- EDIT: onboarding section 2 -->
Included: the major wars and crises, one tight line per development; at
least three significant stories from outside the US, UK and Europe, chosen
for structural importance; disasters as a single roll-up line.

### Economy and markets
<!-- EDIT: onboarding section 3 -->
Included: central bank decisions and signals (Federal Reserve, Bank of
England, European Central Bank); inflation, jobs and growth data against
expectations; sterling and the dollar; what markets are pricing and why;
the artificial intelligence investment cycle as a macro force.
This is the protected core. Full analytical depth, per `house-style.md`.

### Business
<!-- EDIT: onboarding section 4 -->
Included: large company news that says something about a sector; UK
business conditions (costs, tax, regulation); results only when they move
a wider story.
Left out: routine executive moves and appointments; celebrity business.

### Deals and private equity
<!-- EDIT: onboarding section 5 -->
Included: UK and European mid-market and private-equity deals first
(buyouts, take-privates, secondaries, exits, fund raises, financing
conditions); the largest global deals when they say something about the
cycle. Give the deal logic in a clause: why this asset, why now.
Left out: small bolt-ons and routine portfolio sales.

### Science and technology
<!-- EDIT: onboarding section 6 -->
Included: artificial intelligence research and capability; space; biotech
and medicine at the frontier; energy research. Things discovered, proved
or launched. Nothing you can buy.

### Consumer tech
<!-- EDIT: onboarding section 7 -->
Included: major product launches only, each with what it is, what changed,
the price and when it is available. Absent on days nothing shipped.
Left out: rumours, deals, buying guides.

### Energy, climate and infrastructure
<!-- EDIT: onboarding section 8 -->
Included: energy prices and security; climate policy that changes costs;
major infrastructure and construction projects.

### Health, law and society
<!-- EDIT: onboarding section 9 -->
Included: only when something big happens: a landmark ruling, a major
health policy change or clinical result, a demographic shift.

### Culture and sport
<!-- EDIT: onboarding section 10 -->
Included: major sporting results and events; blockbuster film, television
and games releases. No team is followed yet.

### Long reads
<!-- EDIT: the closing section -->
Two picks at most, one line each: feature journalism and ideas pieces worth
the listener's time, especially business and economy stories from outside
the West. Skip crime and corruption exposés.

## Never include

<!-- EDIT: standing exclusions -->
Celebrity news. Scare and tragedy stories with no lasting consequence.
Routine market moves with no cause attached.

## Tone and weather

<!-- EDIT: onboarding step 6 -->
Tone: crisp, analytical, neutral; dry humour in the weather line only.
Weather: on. Two sentences: the next three days, then one joke.
