## PATTERNS for things with no feed of their own
#
# A named company, fund, person or product (put in the lane "Watchlist";
# RARE stops a quiet name being reported as a broken source):
#   Watchlist | GN <Name> | https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=%22<Exact+Name>%22+when%3A14d&hl=en-GB&gl=GB&ceid=GB:en | RARE
#
# A football club (the Guardian has a feed per club):
#   Sport | Guardian <Club> | https://www.theguardian.com/football/<club-slug>/rss |
#
# One publication with no working feed of its own:
#   <Lane> | GN <Site> | https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=site%3A<domain>&hl=en-GB&gl=GB&ceid=GB:en |
#
# Search-based feeds drift: Google quietly ignores words and serves old
# results. Put the key term in intitle: and add when:7d or when:14d, then
# judge the result by how many of the first fifteen items are under two
# days old - not by how many items come back.
