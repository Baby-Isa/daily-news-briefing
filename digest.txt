Deforestation , forest degradation , loss of peatlands and harvesting trees for wood all release carbon into the atmosphere. Each year, global land-use emission trends are analysed in the Global Carbon Budget report. The report, produced by dozens of scientists, documents how human-caused greenhouse gas emissions are changing over time. Key findings from the annual report are released each year in the autumn, before being published formally in an academic journal the following year following a peer-review process.
