What great human and environmental ruptures occurred amid the astonishing material and economic convulsions that forged this city in a garden? These questions are at the center of Ann Durkin Keating’s “Rising Up From Indian Country: The Battle of Fort Dearborn and the Birth of Chicago,” William Cronon’s “Nature’s Metropolis: Chicago and the Great West” and James Green’s “Death in the Haymarket: A Story of Chicago, the First Labor Movement and the Bombing That Divided Gilded Age America.” These historical studies help us make sense of Chicago today, as it stands at the threshold of artificial intelligence and under the boot of Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE).
