The year that has stayed with me is 2004, when he taught what he’d spelled out in Between Facts and Norms (1992), his opus applying his theory to democratic politics. Law, philosophy, history, political science: students spanned many disciplines to come watch the famous German scholar every week, dressed in Wrangler jeans and a tweed jacket, expose his theory of deliberative politics in his unmistakable nasal voice, thick German accent, and impeccable English.
